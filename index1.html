About
Years on stage taught him the repertoire. Leading an orchestra as concertmaster taught him something else — how a season actually gets built, decision by decision, long before an audience takes its seat.
What he leads

From curation to contract, from the empty hall to the sold-out one.

Four areas of work that reinforce one another — none of them functions in isolation.

01

Artistic Direction

Repertoire curation, soloist selection and the musical identity of seasons and festivals, from the first outline to opening night.

02

Concert Production

Planning, budgeting and execution of performances — from chamber recitals to multi-city symphonic programmes.

03

Institutional Partnerships

Coordination between concert halls, sponsors, orchestras and cultural institutions to sustain projects over the medium and long term.

04

Career Guidance

Strategic support for musicians and ensembles building international careers, from touring schedules to public positioning.

Journey

From the stand to the director's desk.

A record built one engagement at a time — each one widening the scope of the next.

Dec 2023

Concertmaster debut

Joins the Strauss Operetta Symphony Orchestra (Vienna Quartiere Artistico) as Concertmaster for the International Tour of New Year's Concerts in China, produced by Hebei Arts Agency Ltd.

Mar 2024

Wiener Konzerthaus, Vienna

Performs as Concertmaster in "In Hope and Memory" at the Wiener Konzerthaus — a programme built around emerging talent alongside internationally acclaimed artists.

Dec 2024 – Jan 2025

Returns to the podium

Reprises the role of Concertmaster for the orchestra's following International Tour of New Year's Concerts in China.

2025

Laureate, Concorso Argento

Recognised as a laureate of the Concorso Argento, 2025 edition.

Today

Building the next chapter

Expanding from performance into artistic direction and concert production — applying the same discipline behind the stand to the decisions behind the season.

How it works

Stage
